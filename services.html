Sewer backup and basement water help in Oakland County

Call (248) 825-8312 for sewer backup cleanup, sewage extraction, flooded basement cleanup, water damage restoration, sump pump repair, or basement sanitization in Royal Oak, Troy, Birmingham, Berkley, or Clawson. Ask the crew for proof of license and insurance before work starts.

Match the water to the right help

If you are not sure whether a drain, a sump, or a storm caused the water, read the county overview first, then your city, so you can describe the source when you call.

Water damage restoration means getting water out, drying what remains, and handling sewage water damage. It is the broadest match for a wet basement.

Sewer backup cleanup means sewage that came up through a drain. Each city page covers the local steps.

Sewage extraction is the pumping and removal step when the water is contaminated.

Flooded basement cleanup is basement water removal when you are dealing with storm water, a window well, or a sump overflow. If a drain was involved, use the sewage pages.

Sump pump repair is the mechanical pump. Overflow cleanup, if the floor is wet, is a water-damage job on top of the repair. A dead pump with a wet floor needs both the repair and the water removal.

Basement sanitization means cleaning after sewage or a contaminated flood. It is not a housekeeping service.

How a call is different from a city sewer report

City public works can look at the main in the street. A local cleanup crew can pump and dry the basement. Call (248) 825-8312 for that crew. If you think the municipal main caused the backup, you still have a separate written-notice deadline under Michigan law. The steps are in the sewer backup claim guide. Storm prep, including the city numbers to keep by the phone, is on the basement flood checklist. Why so many of these basements sit upstream of the Red Run Drain is on the George W. Questions

Which job should I describe when I call?
Whether the water came from a floor drain, a sump, or a storm, and your city. Ask the crew for proof of license and insurance and a written scope.
What kind of cleanup do I need?
If sewage came up a drain, open sewer backup cleanup. If the water is storm or sump water and the drains stayed quiet, open flooded basement cleanup. If materials are already soaked, open water damage restoration. Then open the city page for the house.
Which Oakland County cities can I call about?
Royal Oak, Troy, Birmingham, Berkley, and Clawson. Each has its own sewer description. Berkley's pipe is combined and gravity. Troy discharges through three districts. Birmingham's system is gravity with no city pump stations. Use the city where the house stands.
Is sewage extraction the same as sanitizing?
No. Extraction pumps out the contaminated water and removes soaked material. Sanitizing cleans and disinfects what remains after the water is gone. After a sewer backup you usually need both, in that order.
Is the George W. Kuhn district the same as my city's sewer?
It is the regional district upstream of the Red Run Drain. It serves all or part of 14 communities, including Berkley, Birmingham, Clawson, Royal Oak, and Troy, about 24,500 acres. It does not tell you whether the street main or your lateral failed today. Ask your city.
