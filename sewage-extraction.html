Sewage extraction in Oakland County, MI

Contaminated water is in the basement and it has to be pumped out. Your call connects you with an independent local cleanup company that can come out.

Call (248) 825-8312

What sewage extraction is, and what it is not

Sewage extraction removes contaminated water that left the sanitary plumbing, plus the materials that soaked it up. It is not the sewage cleanup overview, and it is not mopping rain that came through a window. A household vac spreads it. An independent company with the right setup does the removal. Oakland Sewer Pros makes the introduction. The equipment belongs to the company you hire. Call (248) 825-8312 to be connected when a provider is participating.

How removal should be sequenced

The crew should keep the contaminated area from walking into the rest of the house: shoes, hoses, and debris have a path that does not cross the living-room carpet. Standing wastewater is pumped first. Carpet pad, fiberglass insulation, and drywall that has wicked sewage usually come out rather than get dried in place. Hard surfaces that remain can be cleaned afterward. That later step is basement sanitization, not a second pass with the same pump. Ask where the wastewater will be taken. Do not pump it into a street gutter or a storm catch basin yourself.

Safety while the basement is still wet

Stay out if water is near outlets or the furnace. Shut power off only from a dry spot. Stop using water upstairs so toilets and the washer do not add to the backup. Keep children and pets off the stairs. If you feel dizzy or smell a strong sewage odor in a small basement, leave the air to the company that has the equipment. Do not mix bleach with any other cleaner.

What to write down before debris leaves

Photograph each room before pad and drywall are removed. Note the time you discovered the water and whether a floor drain, a toilet, or a standpipe was the source. That timeline matters if you later send the 45-day written notice described in the sewer backup claim guide. Your insurer will ask a different set of questions. Sewer backup is often excluded unless the policy has an endorsement. Ask the carrier. Ask the provider for a written scope and for proof of license and insurance. Oakland Sewer Pros leaves the price and the insurance paperwork with you and the company you hire.

When extraction is the wrong job

If the drains never moved and the water is storm water or a sump overflow, start at flooded basement cleanup. If the pump itself failed, the mechanical page is sump pump repair, and extraction or drying is a second conversation only if the floor got wet. The backup overview, including the regional district, is sewer backup cleanup. Storm steps are on the basement flood checklist. The district map in plain language is the George W. Kuhn Drainage District page.

Pumping help in each city

Sewage extraction in Royal Oak usually means a floor drain in an older house. The city Sewer Division is the public-main contact. Overview: Royal Oak.

Sewage extraction in Troy usually means a split-level lower floor. Troy's wastewater leaves through three districts, so do not assume one pipe type. Overview: Troy.

Sewage extraction in Birmingham has to work around plaster and trim in early- and mid-1900s houses. The city system is gravity and has no municipal pump stations. Overview: Birmingham.

Sewage extraction in Berkley, MI is a short stair in a bungalow on a combined, gravity sewer. Overview: Berkley.

Sewage extraction in Clawson is a compact lot and often a one-room basement. Overview: Clawson.

Questions

Can I shop-vac sewage myself?
No. A household vac sprays contaminated water onto stairs, joists, and anyone standing nearby. Extraction on these pages means an independent company equipped for wastewater.
Does extraction include sanitizing?
Pumping and removing soaked porous materials come first. Cleaning what remains is the basement sanitization page. A fogger is not a substitute for throwing away a soaked pad.
Where should the wastewater go?
The company you hire should say where they will take it. Do not pump sewage into a street gutter or a storm drain yourself.
