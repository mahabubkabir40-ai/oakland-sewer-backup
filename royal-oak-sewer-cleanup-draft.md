## [NEW] What to Do in the First 10 Minutes of a Sewer Backup

If you're dealing with an active backup right now, do this before help arrives:

1. **Stop using water** — don't run faucets, flush toilets, or run the dishwasher/washing machine. This adds more volume to an already overwhelmed line.
2. **Keep people and pets away from the affected area.** Category 3 black water carries pathogens that pose real health risks on contact.
3. **Don't attempt to clear the blockage yourself** with a household plunger or drain snake — this can push contaminated water further into the home or expose you directly to raw sewage.
4. **Shut off electricity to the affected area at the breaker** if it's safe to reach the panel without stepping through standing water.
5. **Call for emergency extraction** — the longer black water sits, the more it saturates drywall, subflooring, and framing, which increases both health risk and repair cost.

## Royal Oak Frequently Asked Questions [EXPANDED — city-specific, non-duplicate]

**Does Royal Oak have a combined sewer system that increases backup risk?**
Many older sections of Royal Oak, like much of Oakland County, were built before separated storm and sanitary sewer systems became standard. In areas with combined or aging infrastructure, heavy rain events put extra strain on the system, which raises the chance of backups reaching residential basements. If you're unsure which type of system serves your specific street, Royal Oak's public works department can confirm.

**What does sewage cleanup typically cost in Royal Oak?**
Costs vary significantly based on how much wastewater entered the home, how long it sat before extraction, and whether structural materials like drywall or flooring need to be removed. Minor backups caught early can run in the low hundreds; extensive black-water damage requiring structural drying and material replacement can run into the thousands. Our teams provide an on-site assessment and itemized estimate before work begins, and can walk you through what's typically covered under a sewer backup insurance rider.

**How long does a basement take to dry out after a Royal Oak sewer backup?**
Once extraction is complete, structural drying typically takes 3 to 5 days using industrial air movers and dehumidifiers, depending on how long the water was standing and how much of the structure absorbed moisture.
