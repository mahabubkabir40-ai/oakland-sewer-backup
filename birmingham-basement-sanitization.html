Basement Sanitization after Sewage or Flood in Birmingham, MI

Sewage wicked into the plaster and wood in a Birmingham basement, past what a wipe can reach. When you call, you reach a local cleanup crew for basement sanitization in Birmingham, and they'll tell you when they can be there.

Call (248) 825-8312

This page covers cleaning after the water is out: what has to be thrown away, what can be cleaned, and how to keep contamination from moving upstairs.

What gets thrown away and what gets cleaned

  • Carpet, pad, cardboard, and insulation that soaked up sewage are thrown away.
  • Hard surfaces that did not absorb the water, such as bare concrete and metal appliance shells, can be cleaned.
  • Soft furnishings that sat in the water are thrown away rather than sprayed and kept.
  • Ask for a written list of what left the basement. Cleaning does not repair the pipe.

Part of Birmingham drains to the George W. Kuhn district, where wet-weather flow in the shared pipes is typically more than 93 percent stormwater, so what comes up a floor drain in a storm is diluted but still sewage. The city posted an engineer's presentation on the August 24, 2023 rain event.

Sanitizing after a flood or sewage backup in an older Birmingham house

Birmingham basement sanitization is mostly a materials problem in older homes. Plaster, wood base, and built-ins do not behave like modern painted drywall. Sewage that wicked into the bottom of plaster is inside the material. A surface wipe of the paint film leaves the contamination in place and can trap odor. The crew has to say whether that plaster comes off.

This is not a housekeeping visit and not a 'make the lower level smell better' product pitch. If you had a sewer backup, start from sewage extraction and sewer backup cleanup. If the water was a flood without sewage, drying and selective cleaning sit under water damage restoration and basement water removal. Call (248) 825-8312.

Wood trim and what sanitizing cannot promise

  • Unfinished end grain on baseboard that sat in sewage is a poor candidate for saving. Painted face grain may look fine and still be fouled at the cut ends.
  • A closed-cell finish on a floor is different from a bare softwood stair. Ask for a specific answer, not a blanket 'we can save the wood.'
  • Plaster keys that are already soft will not hold because someone sprayed them.
  • Ask the company on site when they will recheck the odor. A date is their call after they see what stayed.

Who should do the work

Hire a restoration company that will put the scope in writing, including what is discarded. A janitorial service with a mop is the wrong trade once sewage is involved. Verify license and insurance for the work. Those documents come from the crew. If a sump pit was part of the contamination, include the pump in the discussion so the crock is not the piece left dirty.

Birmingham: do not fog the house and hope

  • Odor treatments that do not remove the wet material only cover the smell until the next humid day.
  • Keep children out of a lower level that had sewage, even after it looks dry, until the company says the demolition is done.
  • Do not mix random household chemicals in the same bucket.
  • Photograph discarded materials if you plan to talk to your insurer.

Birmingham questions

Can original Birmingham plaster be sanitized in place?
Only if sewage did not soak through it. Soft or deeply stained plaster usually has to be removed. The crew should decide after looking at the room.
Is a deodorant fog enough after a Birmingham backup?
No. Fog does not replace removing the contaminated material. The smell returns when humidity rises.
Is floor-drain water in a heavy Birmingham storm sewage?
Treat it that way. Birmingham's FAQ says its older combined and storm sewers were designed for about 2 inches of rain in one hour, so a heavier storm can push dirty water up a floor drain. Clean anything it touched as sewage. (Source: City of Birmingham Risk Management)
Will Birmingham's backflow advice clean sewage that is already in the plaster?
No. A backflow preventer, downspouts extended about 6 feet, and grading away from the foundation are prevention steps from the city FAQ. Soft plaster that soaked up sewage usually has to come out. The water-event line (248) 530-1703 is not the cleaning crew. (Source: City of Birmingham Risk Management)
Does cleaning Birmingham plaster file the sewer backup claim?
Removing soft plaster does not send the letter. Written notice is due within 45 days of discovery, on the city's sewer backup claim form. The water-event form is not that claim. Claims questions are 248.530.1808. The city says claims go through the Michigan Municipal League Liability and Property Pool and Meadowbrook Claims Service. (Source: City of Birmingham Risk Management)
