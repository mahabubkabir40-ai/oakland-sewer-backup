Why Berkley homes are at higher risk

Berkley's housing stock is largely bungalow-style construction from the 1940s and 1950s, built with original clay or cast-iron sewer laterals that are reaching the end of their service life. Berkley sits within the Rouge River watershed, and its relatively flat topography means heavy rain has limited places to drain quickly. Combined with older municipal combined sewer lines, heavy rain events push extra wastewater volume back up through basement floor drains in older homes.

Common backup triggers in Berkley

  • Backups tied to older sections that still carry storm and sanitary flow together during heavy rain
  • Root intrusion in clay sewer laterals common to 1940s–50s bungalow construction
  • Sump pump overload during downpours in this part of the Rouge River watershed
  • Water reaching original hardwood floors and framing in older homes

What to do in the first 10 minutes of a sewer backup in Berkley

  1. Stop using water. Do not run faucets, flush toilets, or run the washer or dishwasher.
  2. Keep people and pets out of the water. Sewage carries bacteria and other pathogens.
  3. Do not plunge or snake the drain. A household snake can push dirty water into the subfloor.
  4. Leave the basement if water is near outlets, the panel, or the furnace. Shut power off only from a dry location.
  5. Call Berkley help at the number above to be connected with an independent provider. How fast someone arrives depends on who is available. This site does not promise a response time.

Berkley questions

Does Berkley still have combined sewer sections?
Parts of Berkley's older system have been described as carrying storm and sanitary flow together. During hard rain that shared capacity can push water back through basement drains. Confirm the pipe in front of your house with the city. Treat this as a reason to ask, not as a map.
Who handles sewage extraction in a Berkley bungalow?

Why is a shop vac a poor idea for Berkley sewage water?
A household vac aerosolizes contaminated water in a small basement and on a short stair. That is a health problem in a bungalow where the stairs open near living space. Wait for a company equipped for sewage.
Is sewer-backup damage covered on a Berkley homeowners policy?
