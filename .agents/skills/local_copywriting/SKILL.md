# Copywriting Skill

This skill details copywriting guidelines to produce content that ranks highly on search engines while maximizing conversion rates for emergency sewer backup and water restoration clients.

## Copywriting Framework

1. **Avoid Keyword Stuffing:**
   - Write naturally for human users first. Keep keyword density between **1% and 1.5%**.
   - Place target keyword phrases in primary spots (H1, introductory paragraph, H2, and title). Do not repeat keywords artificially.

2. **Match Search Intent:**
   - **Transactional Intent** (`sewage backup cleanup [Suburb]`, `sump pump repair`): Put click-to-call buttons and the phone number `(248) 825-8312` front-and-center.
   - **Commercial Intent** (`sewer cleanup cost`): Focus heavily on protecting finished basements (gyms, theaters, family rooms) which have high insurance payouts. Explain that we work directly with major insurance providers.
   - **Informational Intent** (`why is sewage backup dangerous`): Explain class 3 black water hazards, structural damage, mold growth timeline (24-48 hours).

3. **Incorporate Local Signals:**
   - Mention local landmarks, county names, neighboring towns, or major local streets (e.g. Woodward Ave, Somerset Collection, local parks) to build hyper-local search relevance in Oakland County, Michigan.
